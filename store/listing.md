## Full description (max 4000)
You can't attack. They can.

Fly through a drifting asteroid field ahead of a death wave, with missiles locked on your tail. They're faster than you but turn wider, so lead them into rocks, walls, mines, lasers and each other. Every hit explodes and sets off the ones nearby. Pack a crowd tight and watch the chain go up.

Let a missile roar past within a hair and time slows for a breath. Earn it again.

DAILY CHALLENGE
• One course a day, the same for everyone.
• Beat your best score, with your ghost flying beside you.
• Keep your streak alive.

• One thumb: put it down anywhere and steer.
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
• Works offline.
