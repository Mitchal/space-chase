Space Chase: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Space Chase is a single-player game with an optional online leaderboard for the daily challenges. The only thing that ever leaves your device is what you choose to put on the leaderboard: a nickname and your scores.

What the game stores on your device

Your scores, today's best run (the "ghost"), your day streak, your settings and the leaderboard nickname you picked are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes them.

The leaderboard

The main menu and the end of each daily run show today's leaderboard, and a calendar shows the top five of any past day. To get them, the game asks the leaderboard server for the top scores. Those requests carry no personal data.

The leaderboard is opt-in. Only if you type a nickname to join it does the game send, after each daily run:

  • the nickname you typed,
  • which daily challenge it was and the date,
  • your best score in that challenge that day,
  • a recording of how you steered in that run (which way, moment by moment: nothing else), so the score can be checked by playing the run again.

Your nickname and scores are public: anyone who plays can see them. Pick a nickname, not your real name. Changing your nickname moves today's scores to the new one.

Once you've joined, the game also asks the server which trophies your nickname has won (gold, silver and bronze for a day's top three), to show them on the main menu.

Nicknames that are obviously offensive are refused, and others may be removed.

The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare. Like any server on the internet, it sees the IP address a request comes from in order to answer it, and it counts requests per address for a minute to stop floods. The game does not store IP addresses or link them to nicknames.

Scores are checked a few minutes after they arrive, on GitHub: the server hands GitHub the steering recording with the challenge, date and score (not your nickname), GitHub plays the run again, and the recording is deleted from the server once the score checks out. A score that doesn't check out is hidden from the leaderboard; its recording is kept with it as evidence until it's deleted with the rest of that day's scores.

Scores and nicknames are deleted automatically after 30 days, except each day's top five in each challenge: those are kept, with their nicknames, for the calendar of past days and the trophies. To have a nickname and its scores removed, including from past days, email the address below.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email addresses or real names.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No network requests while you fly. The game works offline; without a connection the leaderboard simply doesn't show.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a run, the game hands a short line of text (your score and the date) to your device's share menu or clipboard. You choose where it goes. The game itself sends nothing.

Children

The game asks no one, including children, for personal information. The leaderboard nickname is shown to everyone, so children should pick one that isn't their real name, or not join the leaderboard.

Changes

If what the game sends ever changes, this page will be updated before that version is released.
