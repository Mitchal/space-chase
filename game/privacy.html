Space Chase: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Space Chase is a single-player game. It does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Your scores, today's best run (the "ghost"), your day streak and your settings are stored only on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal information.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No network requests while you play. The Android app works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a run, the game hands a short line of text (your score and the date) to your device's share menu or clipboard. You choose where it goes. The game itself sends nothing.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, an online leaderboard), this page will be updated before that version is released.
