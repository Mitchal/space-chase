# Lure v2: what changed and why

Every change traces to a pillar in `vision.md`. v1 is preserved at `game/v1.html`.

| Change | Pillar | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Chasers fast but wide-turning | P2 high octane, readable motion | speed 168 (v1-A 135, v1-B 175); turn rate 2.3 rad/s (v1-A 2.0, v1-B 4.2) |

## The blind A/B in v2
A = no dash. B = dash (Shift/Space: a short burst, 2.2s cooldown, no invulnerability).
Question: does a dash amplify the reference moment (the last-second spin) or cheapen the threat?
Note: unlike v1, this variant is visible in play (B shows a dash meter), so it's blind only in that Mikael isn't told the hypothesis.
